PUBLIC EPISODE CATALOGUE AND CODEBOOK
When Assurance Work Displaces the Task
Song Luo | Independent Researcher | 4 November 2026

Scope
The CSV contains 32 reanalysed core episodes (C01-C32) from 20 conversations,
plus three purposive contrast episodes (N01-N03) from three additional
conversations. One episode is a distinguishable task-and-correction sequence.
Entries from the same conversation are dependent; these are not independent
failures, model runs, or experimental replicates.

Fields
episode_id: public identifier retained across the descriptive report and paper.
conversation_alias: non-identifying group label for records in the same chat.
context: generalized task type, with identifying project details removed.
observation: paraphrased activity visible in the retained evidence.
correction: user correction, revised instruction, or subsequent change of route.
recorded_outcome: strongest outcome supported; proposed and reported actions
  are not automatically treated as independently verified completion.
evidence_basis: what kind of retained evidence supports the entry.
interpretive_limit: an alternative explanation, uncertainty, or boundary that
  prevents overreading the episode.

Evidence notation
M = messages, including original user requirements and agent responses.
C = a contemporaneous close-out report of execution; still an agent report.
A = a retained artifact inspected for this investigation. The artifact may
    itself have been produced in AI-assisted work; this is not an external audit.

Working definitions
Overdefensiveness: risk handling, confirmation, protection, or qualification
that exceeds the demands of the current task, actual authorization, and concrete
risk. A valid safeguard is not excess solely because it is inconvenient.

Overengineering: structure, functionality, process, or verification whose
additional cost is not adequately connected to a present requirement, existing
contract, or observed failure. Size and number of calls are insufficient alone.

Adjacent failures: inaccurate status, scope drift, premature stopping, omitted
required review, weak synthesis, or unsupported attribution of benefit. These
may coexist with excess but are not interchangeable with it.

Interpretation procedure
Read the requested result and relevant authorization before judging an addition.
Distinguish a proposal, authorization, execution report, observed artifact, and
user acceptance. Ask what the addition changes for the required result, and
whether removing it would violate a requirement. Preserve justified safeguards
and unresolved judgments. An apology or a complaint alone is not a verdict.

Reanalysis differences
The manuscript does not carry forward a binary failure count from the earlier
descriptive report. In C06 the initial small governance tool was requested; the
later proposed expansion was reconsidered. In C19 personal reading was first
approved and then replaced by explicitly requested model review. These are
important authorization changes. Other entries retain neighboring failures and
boundary cases, including C12, C16, C22, C27, C28, and C32.

The earlier inventory-based candidate count was 344. The saved searchable
message extracts used for the manuscript contain 343 distinct conversation IDs
(315 from session extraction and 28 from database recovery, without overlap).
Neither is a prevalence denominator. The initial inventory also contains agent
and approval sessions; session type alone does not establish a human task.

Contrast selection
The three additional entries were purposively selected outside the 20 core
conversations for directly inspectable request/response boundaries. Short
scripted research assignments were excluded from that contrast selection.
They are not matched controls or a systematic sample of normal work. Their
format compliance does not establish source accuracy, professional validity,
user acceptance, or an overall task success rate.

Privacy and availability
All public reconstructions are paraphrases, not verbatim quotations. The author
is the participant and is identified; individual task and third-party details
are deidentified. Raw logs, exact dates, paths, private identifiers, credentials,
and the source crosswalk are withheld. Access to private records is not promised.
The catalogue supports scrutiny of the published account, not full independent
reproduction of raw-data coding. No independent human coding or new controlled
experiment was performed. Codex assisted analysis and writing.
