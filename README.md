# When Assurance Work Displaces the Task

**A Retrospective Case Study of Overdefensiveness and Overengineering in AI Agent Use**

Song Luo · Independent Researcher · 4 November 2026 · Version 1.0

[Read the paper](When_Assurance_Work_Displaces_the_Task.pdf) · [Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/records/23940714) · [DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23940714](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23940714)

AI agents can add confirmation, checks, rules, and infrastructure while losing sight of the requested result. This preprint examines how that happens through **35 task-and-correction episodes from 23 Codex conversations**: 32 reanalysed core episodes and three additional contrast episodes.

## What the study finds

- **Overdefensiveness and overengineering are distinct.** The former concerns protection, confirmation, and qualification beyond the task's actual needs and authorization; the latter concerns complexity without a sufficiently demonstrated use.
- **Process success can miss task success.** A manually refreshed page does not verify scheduled operation. A valid verification record does not by itself establish an additional prevented error.
- **Concrete corrections have visible local effects.** Restoring the deliverable, recognizing existing capabilities, removing low-value additions, and checking the actual use path can resolve particular mismatches.
- **Authorization changes the interpretation.** Some substantial assurance work was explicitly requested and later reconsidered. Necessary safeguards and required independent review remain legitimate.

The paper develops an interpretive account of how assurance work can become its own object of evaluation, plus three propositions for future study.

This is a single-participant, retrospective, AI-assisted case study. The entries are not independent failures or experimental replications. The study does not estimate prevalence, establish that a prompt produces durable improvement, or rank models and harnesses. It has not been peer reviewed.

The author is also the participant. Codex assisted retrieval, analysis, drafting, and document production. Public case descriptions are deidentified paraphrases. Raw conversations, private artifacts, and the identifying source crosswalk are withheld.

## 中文介绍

这篇论文研究 AI 智能体使用中的过度防御与过度工程化：为什么确认、检查、规则和工具有时会挤占原本的任务，以及哪些具体纠正产生了可观察的局部改善。

研究回看 23 段 Codex 对话中的 35 个案例片段，区分多余步骤、必要保护、遗漏交付和用户中途调整要求等情况。公开材料包括英文论文、脱敏案例目录及字段说明。它是一项单用户回顾性案例研究，长期干预效果仍需进一步验证。
